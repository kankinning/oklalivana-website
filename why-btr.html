Why BTR
What is The Build to Rent Advantage
Designed for long-term comfort, community, and convenience, Build to Rent reimagines what it means to rent in New Zealand—offering secure, high-quality homes with professional management in well-connected neighbourhoods like Albany. BTR brings a new standard of rental living, backed by reliable service, lasting tenancies, and a community-first design ethos.
This model redefines renting: tenants are seen as valued customers, and the living experience is the product. From on-site maintenance teams to communal lounges. BTR developments offer a seamless blend of service and comfort.
Sustainability is also central to the BTR philosophy, with many projects aiming for high Homestar and Green Star ratings through environmentally conscious construction methods and energy-efficient design. By focusing development around existing infrastructure and public transit, BTR also contributes to more sustainable urban growth.

01What is Build to Rent
What is Build to Rent
Build to Rent (BTR) developments are large-scale residential communities, typically held by institutional investors and managed by dedicated, professional operators. These properties are purpose-built to deliver long-term rental housing with consistent quality and a tenant-first approach. Unlike traditional rentals, BTR represents a new model of “commercial living” – often located near major urban hubs or key transport nodes, designed for convenience, flexibility, and lifestyle.
02The New Law
Game-changer: New Law Invites Overseas Investment into New Zealand's BTR Sector
In a significant move to address the housing crisis and attract foreign capital, the New Zealand government has introduced legislation (Overseas Investment (Build-to-rent and Similar Rental Developments) Amendment Act 2025) allowing overseas investors to fund and develop in the Build to Rent (BTR) sector. This legislative development marks a pivotal step in transforming the nation's rental landscape, boosting housing supply, attracting capital and addressing the ongoing demand for affordable, high-quality housing.
03The Rental Experience
Elevating the Rental Experience
Each community is professionally managed, often featuring dedicated onsite teams and impressive shared amenities. In Build to Rent, renters are valued clients, so maintenance requests are handled swiftly and professionally. You’ll never have to chase a landlord again over minor repairs. Unlike traditional rentals, BTR properties prioritize tenant satisfaction, ensuring prompt maintenance, flexible living arrangements, and the freedom to personalize one's space.
04BTR in New Zealand
The Rise of BTR in New Zealand
As New Zealand faces a critical housing shortage and a pressing demand for warm, dry, and healthy rental homes, the property sector has taken steps to initiate the Build to Rent movement. This new model aims to reshape and elevate the rental experience nationwide. Recent legislative changes have further supported the growth of BTR, making it a viable and attractive option for both tenants and investors.
