Kingsman Development ( LIIG Group )
ABOUT
At Kingsman Development ( LIIG Group ), we bring together vision, capability, and experience to deliver landmark residential communities that redefine urban living. With a proven track record in New Zealand’s living-sector, our team is proud to lead the next generation of Build-to-Rent development in Auckland.
01Track Record
A Decade of Proven Capability in The Living-Sector
With over 10 years of extensive experience in medium- to large-scale residential development across Auckland, Kingsman Development has earned a reputation for delivering high-quality, design-led residential developments. Our expertise spans the full development lifecycle from land acquisition to delivery. Our focus on Build-to-Rent reflects our belief in the long-term value of community-centred, professionally managed rental living.
Award-Winning Delivery Track Record
Our projects are executed with excellence. Our most recent success, The O.K.L.A. development, received the Excellence Award from the New Zealand Property Council in 2021, highlighting the quality of our work and our contribution to Auckland’s built environment. From site acquisition to completion, our methodology is disciplined, efficient, and outcome-driven. This recognition is a testament to our pursuit of design innovation, construction quality, and long-term livability.
02People & Partners
A High-Performance Team With Deep Roots in Auckland
Led by co-founders Siyang Li and Chen Kan, Our directors bring decades of combined experience across architecture, development management, project delivery and finance, allowing us to respond quickly to market opportunities. From market positioning to risk management and feasibility analysis, our directors personally drive every phase of the development process. We are locally based and deeply connected. Our hands-on approach ensures clarity, consistency, and control at every stage of the development process.
Powered by an Elite Consultant Network
We collaborate closely with New Zealand’s top-tier consultants, builders, suppliers, and technical specialists, ensuring that every aspect of our developments is held to the highest standard. From renowned firm Haydn & Rollett as our build partner, to a trusted network of planners, architects, engineers, project managers, marketing agencies, local authorities, funding partners, and legal advisors, we operate with precision and expertise, ensuring timely delivery and uncompromising quality, underpinned by deep local knowledge and established connections.
03Pipeline & Innovation
Driving Innovation in the next generation of BTR Living
Kingsman Development is at the forefront of Auckland’s BTR evolution. We are not just building property, we are building a new way of urban living. From product design to tenant experience, we embrace innovation in materials, sustainability, and long-term rental performance. O.K.L.A. Build to Rent is a flagship step in our broader vision for scalable, resilient, transport-oriented ( TOD ) and community-focused urban development.
A Strategic Pipeline Built for Scale
OKLA is the first in a planned series of Build to Rent projects by Kingsman, forming part of a broader living sector strategy underpinned by high-quality landholdings in prime locations. With over 700+ rental units identified across future stages, our secured pipeline offers scale, continuity, and long-term vision—key attributes for institutional capital seeking exposure to next-generation rental housing.
Experience in Commercial and Mixed-Use Development
Alongside our residential initiatives, Kingsman has been involved in a number of commercial and mixed-use development opportunities in Albany. These projects have included office, retail, showroom, and integrated precincts with a focus on practical master planning, long-term flexibility, and future tenant value. Our capability with design-build and design-lease-own models supports a holistic development approach, balancing architectural form with commercial function in growing urban environments.
04Placemaking in Albany
FIELDS Café: Community-making in Practice
At Kingsman, we believe that great places begin by building meaningful community spaces. FIELDS Café, our flagship community eatery in Albany, is more than a hospitality venue; It is a living expression of our placemaking values. Built on land earmarked for future residential development, FIELDS serves as a testbed for how space, culture, and connection intersect. Today, it is a local landmark and social anchor.
Rooted in Albany, Designed for Togetherness
By creating a destination that reflects Albany’s heritage and evolving lifestyle, we are shaping not just projects, but communities. FIELDS is our first step in proving that thoughtful development can start with something as simple — and powerful — as a place to gather.
