About Livana Co.
Livana Co. is a professional Build-to-Rent (BTR) operator. We do not engage in property speculation or build-to-sell development. We operate long-term rental housing, built around a fully integrated, professionally managed BTR platform.
Build-to-Rent is fundamentally different from traditional residential property management. It is a long-term, income-generating rental model, professionally managed at scale to deliver consistent outcomes for both residents and owners.
Livana Co. brings together expertise across Build-to-Rent operations, residential development, hotel and hospitality asset management, and purpose-built student accommodation, forming a multi-disciplinary, institutionally governed operating platform.

The Underwriting Limited Partnership
A Coordinated Structure
The OKLA · Livana Build-to-Rent project connects three parties around one asset: the Developer, who designs and builds the building; the BTR Manager, who operates it long-term; and the Underwriting Limited Partnership, the vehicle that coordinates investor participation and settlement.
Individual investors enter into direct agreements with the Developer, while their capital commitment is coordinated through the Underwriting Limited Partnership. Once the building is complete, all units are pooled into one professionally managed Build-to-Rent business, operated by Livana Co.
