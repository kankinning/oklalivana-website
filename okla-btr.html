Function Maximised: Layered Logic, Form Serves Longevity
At O.K.L.A. Build to Rent, high performance begins with intelligent spatial planning. Every layer of the development has been purposefully structured to maximise operational clarity, privacy, and long-term functionality.
The ground level is activated by retail tenancies and dedicated visitor parking. Above this sits a secure parking level reserved exclusively for residents, creating a natural buffer that separates public activity from private living zones. Only above this threshold do the rental apartments begin, forming a vertical hierarchy that enhances noise control, safety, and resident experience.
Beyond current delivery, O.K.L.A. has also been masterplanned with scale in mind. Our Phase 1 includes 203 rental units, but the architecture, infrastructure, and site interface have been designed to seamlessly accommodate a future second stage.
For institutional investors, this represents more than a standalone asset. It is a fully integrated pipeline, built with foresight, scalability, and portfolio-level potential, a defining characteristic of globally successful BTR strategies.
Tranquillity in the Heart of Albany
O.K.L.A. Build to Rent sits at the epicentre of Albany’s metropolitan energy, surrounded by world-class retail, transport, education, and civic infrastructure. Yet just beyond the architecture lies a different kind of richness: uninterrupted views across Hooton Reserve that shift with the seasons.
From the deep greens of summer to the golds and reds of autumn, from crisp blue skies to misty winter mornings, this ever-changing natural backdrop offers a moment of stillness in a fast-moving city. Residents enjoy the rare privilege of living in a place where lifestyle meets landscape, where everyday convenience is matched by everyday calm.
04Unit Typology
Unit Typology Summary — Efficient, Innovative & Market-Aligned
The success of a Build-to-Rent (BTR) project hinges critically on the efficiency and market suitability of its unit typologies. O.K.L.A. Build to Rent has been meticulously designed to offer a highly efficient housing mix, with 81.5% of the total Gross Floor Area (GFA) dedicated to rentable space. This ensures maximum utilisation of the building’s footprint, optimizing both tenant appeal and investment performance.
An impressive 90% of the units fall below 72 square meters, a deliberate strategy to cater to the growing demand for high-quality, affordable, and highly functional purpose-built rental accommodation in Albany, one of Auckland’s fastest-growing suburbs.
O.K.L.A. Build to Rent introduces 65% inaugural unit types, never before approved on the local market, yet proven successful internationally. Among these, the DUALKEY units stand out as a game-changer, offering tenants comfortable, self-contained, and flexible apartments while significantly enhancing rental yield as an investment.
